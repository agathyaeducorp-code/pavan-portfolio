Who's behind the work?

I didn't plan the path. I built it.

Curiosity → Design → Freelancing → Branding → Strategy → Business → Building

Curiosity led me toward design — first Canva, then Photoshop, Illustrator and Affinity. College presentations became practical work. My first logo client came when I was 18.

Freelancing expanded into logos, websites, branding and strategy. That practice became Pizhai.

Work at AISECT sharpened sales, partnerships, conversion and stakeholder management. Agathya expanded the work into education, curriculum and business building.

The current intersection is brand + business + technology + building.
