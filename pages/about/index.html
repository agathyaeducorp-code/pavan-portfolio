02 / 08 Founder Story

From making visuals
to building brands.

I started with design because I enjoyed making things. Canva was the beginning, followed by Photoshop, Illustrator and Affinity.

College presentations gave me a reason to keep improving. That work eventually moved outside college, and my first logo client came when I was 18.

Over time, the work expanded from logos and social media into websites, brand identity, strategy and business development.

That progression became the foundation for Pizhai.
