Thinking

Notes from the work, not a content engine.

This page holds the ideas that sit underneath Pizhai: strategy, identity, business, marketing and digital experience. It is built to take future essays without changing the architecture.

A working belief

Strategy before aesthetics.

Good design gets attention. Good strategy gives a brand a direction that can survive contact with the market.

Current ideas

Brand strategy

Positioning, architecture and the reason a brand should exist before it is dressed.

Brand identity

Systems that can be repeated: type, colour, mark, art direction. Not a single hero lockup.

Business

Partnerships, conversion, stakeholders. A brand that cannot move a business is incomplete.

Digital experience

The website, the product surface, the content system. Where the brand is actually used.

An idea I'm developing

Beyond marketing silos.

I am developing a way of thinking about branding and marketing that brings these layers closer together. It is a working framework, not an industry methodology I claim to have established.
